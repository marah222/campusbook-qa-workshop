# CampusBook — Client request (read aloud at 00:00)

> We need a simple way for university students to find and reserve available study rooms. Students should be able to manage their reservations easily, and the system should prevent booking problems.

**Your mission:** Decide what the system should do, design and execute tests, automate important checks, report defects, and make a release recommendation.

You are assigned one feature and one story objective. **Write the actual story and acceptance criteria independently on paper.** Ask the instructor, acting as Product Owner, to clarify unclear rules. The running application is not a specification; it may behave incorrectly.

- Team A: Room Discovery — S01–S03
- Team B: Room Booking — S04–S06
- Team C: Cancellation — S07–S09
- Team D: My Reservations — S10–S11

**No generative AI, coding, or source inspection during the initial story-writing phase.**
